# Customer 360° Intelligence & Churn Prevention Platform

## 1. Project Overview

Customer 360° Intelligence & Churn Prevention Platform is an
industry-oriented data analytics solution designed to understand
customer behaviour, identify customers at risk of churn, and support
data-driven customer retention decisions.

## 2. Business Context

NovaMart is an omnichannel retail and e-commerce company operating
through its website, mobile application, and physical stores.

The company generates customer data from multiple business functions,
including sales, payments, product purchases, returns, marketing,
customer support, and digital behaviour.

Currently, this information is distributed across different systems,
making it difficult for management and analysts to obtain a complete
view of individual customers.

## 3. Business Problem

NovaMart wants to understand:

- Which customers are most valuable?
- Which customers are showing signs of disengagement?
- Which customers are at higher risk of churn?
- What behavioural patterns are associated with churn?
- How much revenue is associated with high-risk customers?
- Which customer segments require retention efforts?
- What actions could potentially improve customer retention?

## 4. Project Objective

The objective is to build an end-to-end analytics platform that
integrates customer information from multiple sources, analyses
customer behaviour, identifies churn-risk patterns, and presents
actionable business insights through analytical and BI tools.

## 5. Expected Business Outcome

The platform should help NovaMart:

- Understand customers through a unified Customer 360° view.
- Identify valuable and at-risk customer segments.
- Understand factors associated with customer churn.
- Monitor important customer and business KPIs.
- Support data-driven retention decisions.
- Improve visibility into customer behaviour and revenue risk.
